My Top 4 Personal Values

Honesty

Why is this important to you?

Honesty is important to me because the people I keep closest to me in my life. When I lie, even if it's to someone else, it makes me view life differently as a person and sometimes even lose respect for myself.

How did you develop this value?

I came from a household where confessing to something rather than lying got you further and helped you get less punishment. That carried over into my personal life.

Hard Work

Why is this important to you?

I believe anyone who wants to be successful in life needs to be a hard worker. If you’re lazy, it eventually catches up to you.

How did you develop this value?

Being an athlete growing up taught me that effort matters. That work ethic carried over into my job, school, and relationships.

Discipline

Why is this important to you?

Discipline is important because being consistent matters as much as working hard. People with discipline can always be counted on.

How did you develop this value?

Sports helped me learn to love the process over results. Showing up every day made me better even if I didn’t see results right away.

Confidence

Why is this important to you?

Confidence ties in with discipline and hard work. When you do those things, you naturally take pride and believe in yourself.

How did you develop this value?

I developed confidence by preparing, working hard, and believing in myself. Confidence shows how much you value yourself and your goals.
